Privacy policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Jarvis is a personal app used only by its owner. It is not available to other users.

What Google data Jarvis accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): to read specific notification emails (for example, utility bills) in the owner's own mailbox. Jarvis cannot send, change or delete email.
  • Google Sheets: to write status rows to a spreadsheet the owner owns.

How the data is used and stored

Data is processed and stored only on the owner's own computer, and access tokens are kept in that computer's secure keychain. Jarvis does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on Jarvis's Google sign-in screen.
