Jarvis

A personal home-automation dashboard

Jarvis is a private, self-hosted automation hub that runs on its owner's own computer. It is used by one person only and is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, Jarvis reads the owner's Gmail (read-only) to pick up things like utility bills, and writes status rows to a Google Sheet the owner owns. All data stays on the owner's computer and in the owner's own Google account.
